# Afonso Portela

**BIM Information Manager and HBIM specialist.** I turn laser scans and drone photogrammetry into information-rich models of historic buildings and infrastructure, and build the tools that put them on the web.

Founder of [XYZ BIM](https://xyzbim.eu). Working between Figueira da Foz (PT) and Germany.

---

### What I work on

- **Scan-to-BIM / HBIM**: point clouds to IFC models of heritage buildings and infrastructure
- **IFC tooling**: IfcOpenShell scripting, IFC4 to IFC4.3 migration, ISO 19650 information management
- **Web viewers**: point clouds, Gaussian splats and BIM in the browser, see [pointcloud.xyzbim.eu](https://pointcloud.xyzbim.eu)
- **Photogrammetry pipelines**: drone capture to mesh, splat and viewer-ready data

### See it live

- [xyzbim.eu](https://xyzbim.eu): practice site, heritage surveys and HBIM models
- [pointcloud.xyzbim.eu](https://pointcloud.xyzbim.eu): browser viewer for point clouds and splats
